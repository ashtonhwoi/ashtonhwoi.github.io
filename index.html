What I do

AI video production

Product photos become reference stills, then multi-shot prompts, then video, then a clean edit with captions and sound. I use stills to check the direction before generating video, then review and refine the result.

Workflows with Codex & Claude Code

I turn repeated work into reusable skills: creative briefs, research, planning and video production. I work with Codex and Claude Code, keeping references, decisions and checks together so a workflow can be used again.

Teaching what works

Through Phantom University and project tutorials, I share how I build: reference images, prompt choices, production steps and the mistakes I learn from.

Selected work

STUSY · IN DEVELOPMENT

From a crowded syllabus to a study plan

A study-planning project I’m building with a partner. The current prototype is a reusable SKILL.md: it guides students through their syllabuses, confirms assessment dates and weights, and produces a weekly plan and calendar export. Revision questions use the student’s own uploaded notes.

I’ve worked on the tutorial, survey and feedback materials, and a character-led promo. For the promo, I developed two fixed character references, separated a 12-panel storyboard, assembled three generated clips into a 36-second draft, and packaged the production process as a reusable Codex skill.

PHANTOM

A production pipeline for AI product videos

A repeatable path from one product photo to a short ad: a product and model card, a storyboard, keyframe stills, a quick animatic, then image-to-video one move per shot. Each step has a check before any video credits are spent.
